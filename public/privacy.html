GDrive Vault Sync – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

GDrive Vault Sync is a personal Obsidian plugin built and used only by Parker Demelia. It copies notes from an Obsidian vault into the user's own Google Drive.

What it accesses

The plugin requests the Google Drive drive.file scope. It can only see, create, change and delete the files and folders it creates itself. It cannot see any other files in your Google Drive. It also reads your Google account email address so it can show which account is connected.

Where data goes

Notes travel directly between the Obsidian app on your device and Google's servers. No other server is involved. No data is collected, sold, shared or sent to anyone else, and there is no analytics or tracking.

Where credentials are stored

Google sign-in tokens are stored only in the plugin's settings file inside your own vault. You can revoke access at any time from the plugin's settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
