by night? i travel various latin american countries
by day? i teach the language to my local high school students

very quickly, i encountered a problem.
duolingo is cool, but its not *that* cool. 
kahoot is fun, but it's not *that* fun.
and voces just flat out sucks.

so i took to my good friend claude and we did something about it. 

Introducing [¡JuegaEspanol!](https://www.juegaespanol.com/), an all-in-one solution for learning the Spanish language, and deploying it to students.

Our **Learn** platform contains duolingo-style lessons, stories (with comprehensive quiz material and study guides), vocab games, live-classroom play, and much more. 

![[juegaespanol-learn.webp|the Learn side: lessons, stories, games and live play]]

Our **Teach** platform builds a classroom management layer overtop of Learn, enabling teachers and administrators to create classes (auto-synced from Google Classroom), assign work, and see student grades. 

![[juegaespanol-teach.webp|the Teach side: one dashboard for the whole class]]

At a later date i'll get into the technical aspects of the platform, but for now, check it out and let me know what you think! If you are a teacher, [contact me](mailto:parker@juegaespanol.com) and i'll add you to our teaching whitelist.
