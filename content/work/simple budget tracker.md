at the start of my backpacking trip, I made a goal to track my expenses for the entire trip, and this is the solution I came up with:
in short:
- notion database -> purchase entries
- text message to ai -> insert entry
- data sorting on website -> view spending at a glance

1. Notion Database with fields
	1. Title, Price, Date, Category, Location
2. Sign up for [Poke](https://poke.com/)
	1. Connect Notion Integration
	2. Brief with "when I say a price and item, add to notion database for expenses"
	3. **On its own, this is done! You can freely track expenses.**
