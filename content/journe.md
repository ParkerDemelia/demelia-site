it took me 5 days on the road in my cramped subaru legacy to decide i was going to buy a van. it took me less than 2 weeks to buy one. i didn't even see it before i pulled the trigger. 

now here's my story. 

7/18 DAY 1 - THE JOURNE BEGINS

i purchased a 1997 Ford e350 Club Wagon {$2000} with 108,067 miles on it. The previous owner (lets call him Dave), had done quite a bit of work: electrical all throughout, wooden cabinets, insulated floor and ceiling, the whole nine yards.... read about it [here](/build).

this was quite a bit, but it wasn't doing it for me.

so I tore it apart. Literally. Within 2 weeks I had a mess of wires, screws, insulation, and boards filling my dads garage as I decided what was next.

9/26 next would be the fryburg fair... in 2 weeks!

every panel i tore off had to be put back on for this one. i scoured marketplace for items that i thought would fit the build, like a 12v fridge and toilet.

10/8-10/9 FIRST CAMP - FRYBURG FAIR @ Fryeburg, ME


driving the van for the first time was horrifying. it's all over the road, i missed so many turns, and my dash kept freaking out, but we made it. 

my sister joined for the night, keeping me company as i opened this new chapter.
